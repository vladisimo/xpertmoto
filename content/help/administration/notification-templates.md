# Notification templates

Notification templates are the standard emails and SMS the system sends
customers automatically — booking confirmations, reminders, receipts, review
requests and more. This is where you edit their wording so they match your voice
and policies.

## Editing a template

1. Open **Notification templates** and pick the message to edit.
2. Update the content. Templates use **placeholders** (the customer's name, the
   booking reference, dates, amounts) that are filled in at send time — keep
   those intact so each message is personalised.
3. Save. New sends use the updated wording immediately.

## What to keep right

- **Branding** — trading name, legal name and ABN come from configuration, so you
  don't hardcode them into a template. Refer to the brand generically and let the
  system fill it in.
- **Policy accuracy** — if a template states a cancellation window or bond
  timeframe, make sure it matches the real settings (see **Cancellation &
  no-show policy** and **Bonds & deposits**). A mismatch creates disputes.
- **Compliance** — keep required information (ABN on receipts, opt-out on
  marketing) in place.

## Templates vs. one-off messages

These templates drive *automated* and *transactional* messages. For a personal,
one-off note to a customer, staff use **Compose** in the Operations
**Communications** area instead.

## After editing

Send yourself a test where the editor supports it, and check the **Communications
log** after the next real send to confirm the new copy renders correctly.
