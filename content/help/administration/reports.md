# Reports

Reports is where the summary dashboards give way to detail you can analyse and
export. It's organised by subject.

## The tabs

- **Bookings** — volume, value, sources, conversion and cancellation patterns.
- **Fleet** — utilisation, downtime, revenue per vehicle and category.
- **Customers** — acquisition, repeat rate, loyalty and value.
- **Financial** — revenue, GST, refunds, debt and reconciliation-level figures.
- **Operational** — throughput, overdue rates, settlement and exceptions.

## Using reports well

1. Start from a question ("why did revenue dip last week?") rather than browsing.
2. Pick the matching tab and narrow by date range and depot.
3. Cross-check against **AI Insights** (which often points at the same story in
   plain English) and **Finance** for the authoritative money figures.
4. Export when you need to share or work the numbers elsewhere.

## A note on accuracy

Reports read from the same live data as the rest of the system. If a figure looks
wrong, the cause is usually upstream data — an un-logged maintenance, an
unmatched toll, an unsettled return — rather than the report itself. Trace it back
to the source record.
