# Admin dashboard

The Admin dashboard is your daily business health check — the indigo-portal
counterpart to the Operations dashboard. Where Operations is about *today's
work*, this is about *the state of the business*.

## The tabs

- **Overview** — headline KPIs: revenue, bookings, utilisation and trend.
- **Risk** — exposure that needs watching: unverified customers, high-value
  hires, prior-damage flags.
- **Debt** — outstanding balances owed to the business; chase these before they
  age. Tied to each booking's balance due.
- **Support** — open ticket volume and themes at a glance.

## How to use it

1. Start on **Overview** for the headline picture.
2. Check **Debt** — overdue balances are the most recoverable money in the
   business, but only while they're fresh.
3. Scan **Risk** so nothing expensive goes out without the right checks.
4. Use **Support** as a quick read on customer friction.

## Going deeper

The dashboard is a summary. For detail and exports, move to **Reports**. For the
money itself, go to **Finance**. For who-did-what, see the **Audit Log**.
