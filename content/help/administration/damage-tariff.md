# Damage tariff

The damage tariff is the priced list of damage items staff use to charge for
damage found when a vehicle is returned. A clear, fair tariff makes settlement
fast and consistent, and removes haggling at the counter.

## What it contains

A set of damage items, each with a description and a price — for example a
scratched panel, a cracked mirror, a damaged seat, a missing key. Staff select
the matching items during **check-in settlement** and the charge is calculated
for them.

## Maintaining it

1. Open **Damage tariff** and review the items.
2. Keep prices aligned to your **real repair/replacement costs** — under-pricing
   leaves the business out of pocket; over-pricing invites disputes and refunds.
3. Add items for damage you see repeatedly so staff aren't improvising prices.

## How it's used at the counter

When staff check a vehicle in, they compare it against the check-out inspection,
pick the relevant tariff items for any new damage, and the total is applied to
the settlement — taken as payment or captured against the **bond**. See
**Checking a vehicle in** and **Bonds & deposits**.

## Fairness

Because every charge traces to a tariff item and is backed by check-out and
check-in **photos**, damage charges are defensible. Keep the tariff current and
the inspections thorough and disputes stay rare.
