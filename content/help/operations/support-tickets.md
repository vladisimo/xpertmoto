# Support tickets

Support is where customer questions and problems land — raised from the customer
portal or on a customer's behalf. The goal is fast, accurate replies and spotting
patterns before they become complaints.

## The tabs

- **Tickets** — the queue of customer conversations. Pick one up, reply, and
  resolve it when it's done.
- **Insights** — AI-assisted analysis of recurring themes across tickets: the
  questions that keep coming up, sentiment, and where customers get stuck.

## Working a ticket

1. Open a ticket from the queue.
2. Read the history and the linked customer / booking for context.
3. Reply clearly. The AI assistant can draft a grounded answer from your
   policies and FAQ — review and edit before sending; you're responsible for what
   goes out.
4. **Resolve** the ticket when the issue is handled.

## Use Insights to fix root causes

If the **Insights** tab shows the same question over and over — "how do I extend
my booking?", "where's my bond refund?" — that's usually a process or content gap.
Fixing the underlying cause (clearer emails, a better automation, a policy tweak)
removes the tickets entirely. Raise recurring themes with a manager.

## Related

- Reach a customer proactively from **Communications**.
- Bond and refund questions are common — point customers (and yourself) at
