# AI insights

AI Insights turns the platform's data into short, plain-English narratives —
so you get the story of the week without building a report. It's available to
managers and admins.

## What it surfaces

- **Trends** — what's moving up or down (bookings, revenue, utilisation).
- **Risks** — things worth attention: rising overdue hires, growing debt,
  under-performing vehicles or depots.
- **Opportunities** — where there's slack to fill or demand to capture.

Each insight is a readable summary backed by the underlying numbers, with a
detail view when you want to dig in.

## How to use it

- Read it at the **start of the week** to orient before diving into **Reports**.
- Treat insights as a **prompt to investigate**, not gospel — they point you at
  where to look. Confirm anything material against the source data in Reports or
  Finance before acting.
- Share notable findings with the team so action follows insight.

## How it works (briefly)

Insights are generated by a language model summarising your real operational
data. The narratives are grounded in that data, but — like any AI — they can
occasionally over-state or miss nuance. The numbers behind them are the
authority; the prose is there to save you time.
