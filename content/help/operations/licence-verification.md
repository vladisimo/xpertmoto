# Licence verification

No vehicle leaves the depot without a verified, valid driver licence. This is a
legal and safety requirement, and it's a manual check a staff member must make —
the system supports you but won't do it for you.

## The process

1. The customer uploads photos of their licence (front and back) during booking
   or at the counter.
2. At check-out, open the booking or the customer's **Verification** tab.
3. **Compare** the uploaded photos against the physical card the customer hands
   you:
   - Does the photo match the person?
   - Do the name and licence number match the booking?
   - Is the licence **current** (not expired)?
   - Is it the right **class** for the vehicle (e.g. a LAMS-approved bike for a
     learner)?
4. If everything matches, **tick verification**. The system records who verified
   it and when.

## Expired and flagged licences

Expired licences are automatically flagged. Do **not** verify or hand over keys
on an expired or mismatched licence — escalate to a manager if the customer
disputes it.

## Why it's strict

The verification record (who, when) is part of your compliance trail. If there's
ever an incident or insurance claim, this is the evidence that the right checks
were made. Treat it as non-negotiable.
