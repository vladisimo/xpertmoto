# Infringements & tolls

When a vehicle picks up a traffic fine or a toll charge, it arrives addressed to
the operator — but the customer who had the bike at the time is responsible.
These two areas help you match each charge to the right hire and on-charge it.

## Infringements (traffic fines)

1. A fine comes in (speeding, parking, etc.) against a vehicle and a date/time.
2. Open **Infringements** and create or match the record to the **booking that
   was active** at that moment.
3. With the responsible customer identified, you can nominate them to the issuing
   authority and on-charge the fine (plus any admin fee per your policy).

## Tolls (eToLL)

1. Toll charges are imported / tracked under **Tolls**.
2. Each toll is matched to the booking active when the vehicle passed the toll
   point.
3. The charge is added to that hire so it can be recovered — usually settled at
   **check-in** or against the bond.

## Getting the match right

The whole process hinges on linking the charge to the **right booking by
time**. If a charge can't be matched, check the vehicle's hire history around
that timestamp. An unmatched fine or toll is money the business eats — and a
nomination deadline you may miss.
