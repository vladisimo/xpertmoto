# Maintenance

Maintenance keeps vehicles safe and roadworthy, and — just as importantly —
keeps a bike that's in the workshop out of the booking pool so it can't be hired
by mistake.

## Scheduling maintenance

1. Open **Maintenance** (or a vehicle's detail page).
2. Create a maintenance record for the vehicle, with the type of work, dates and
   notes.
3. While the record is open, the vehicle is **off hire** — it won't appear as
   available and the system won't allocate it.

## During the work

- Record what was done, parts and costs as you go.
- Keep the expected return-to-service date current so planning stays accurate.

## Returning to service

When the work is finished, close the maintenance record. The vehicle re-enters
the available pool (subject to the usual cleaning buffer and no clashes).

## Why this matters for availability

A vehicle is only bookable when it has **no overlapping maintenance**. That's why
logging maintenance properly is essential — an un-logged service means the system
might cheerfully book out a bike that's actually on the lift. See **Availability
& allocation**.

## Related

- For accidents, theft or damage events, use **Incidents**.
