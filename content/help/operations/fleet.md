# Fleet

The Fleet area is the live inventory of every vehicle {{siteName}} hires out.
Use it to check status, open a vehicle's detail, and understand availability.

## The vehicle list

Each vehicle shows its internal code, rego, make/model/year and current
**status**. Search and filter to find a specific unit, or use **⌘K** to jump
straight to one by rego, code or VIN.

## Vehicle status

A vehicle's status drives whether it can be hired:

- **Available** — ready to go out (subject to the cleaning buffer and no clashes).
- **On hire** — currently with a customer.
- **Maintenance** — in the workshop and off hire; see **Maintenance**.
- Other off-road statuses keep the unit out of the available pool.

See **Availability & allocation** for exactly how the system decides a vehicle
is bookable.

## The vehicle detail page

Open a vehicle to see:

- Its specifications and category (which feeds pricing).
- Hire history and upcoming bookings.
- Maintenance and inspection records.
- Telematics / telemetry data where fitted.

## Keeping the fleet accurate

The fleet view is only as good as the data behind it:

- Flag bikes that need work for **Maintenance** so they leave the available pool.
- Record condition through **Inspections** at check-out and check-in.
- Log damage events as **Incidents**.

An accurate fleet status is what makes availability and allocation trustworthy.
