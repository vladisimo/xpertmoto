# Communications

The Communications area is how {{siteName}} talks to customers by email and SMS —
both the automated messages the system sends and the ones you send by hand. It's
organised into tabs.

## The tabs

- **Log** — every message sent, with delivery status. Start here to confirm a
  customer actually received something.
- **Compose** — send a one-off email or SMS to a customer or a group.
- **Campaigns** — larger, scheduled sends to many customers (promotions,
  announcements).
- **Segments** — saved audiences (e.g. "lapsed customers", "due back this week")
  that campaigns and sends target.
- **Preferences** — channel and opt-in/opt-out settings. Always respected.
- **Automations** — rules that send messages automatically on a trigger (booking
  confirmed, return reminder, review request).

## Sending responsibly

- **Respect preferences.** If a customer has opted out of a channel, the system
  won't send and neither should you.
- **Check the log** if a customer says they didn't get a confirmation before
  re-sending — it may have been delivered, or it may have genuinely failed.
- **Use segments** rather than ad-hoc lists so your audiences stay consistent and
  repeatable.

## Templates vs. composing

The wording of automated messages lives in **Notification templates** (in the
Administration portal) — that's where an admin edits the standard confirmation
and reminder copy. Use **Compose** here for one-off, personal messages.

## If a message fails

A failed send shows in the **Log** with a reason. Some failures are transient
(retry); others point at a bad address or number, or a provider issue an admin
should check under **Integrations → Messaging**.
