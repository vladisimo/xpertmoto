# Inspections

Inspections capture the condition of a vehicle at a point in time, with photos
and damage marks. They're the evidence base for damage settlement and the record
of a vehicle's history.

## When you inspect

- **At check-out** — the baseline condition before the customer takes the bike.
- **At check-in** — the condition on return, compared against the check-out
  baseline.
- **Ad hoc** — after maintenance, or any time you want a condition record.

## Recording an inspection

1. Work around the vehicle systematically (front, sides, rear, controls, tyres).
2. Mark any damage on the diagram and add clear, well-lit **photos**.
3. Note anything that needs follow-up (a service, a charge, an incident).

## Why photos are non-negotiable

A damage charge at return is only fair — and only defensible — if you can show
the bike left clean and came back damaged. The check-out and check-in
inspections together are that proof. Under-photographing at check-out is the most
common reason a damage charge can't be supported later.

## Related

- Damage found at check-in is priced from the **Damage tariff** during settlement
  — see **Checking a vehicle in**.
- Major damage, crashes or theft should also be logged as an **Incident**.
