# Customers

The Customers area is your CRM for everyone who hires from {{siteName}}. Use it
to look someone up, understand their history and risk, and manage their
documents and preferences.

## Finding a customer

- **⌘K** global search is fastest — search by name, email or phone.
- Or open **Customers** and use the directory filters (status, depot, search).

## The customer profile

Each customer's page is organised into tabs:

- **Overview** — contact details, booking history and a snapshot of their value.
- **Verification** — licence photos and verification status. See **Licence
  verification**.
- **Risk** — flags such as expired licences, prior damage, chargebacks or
  no-shows. Check this before high-value hires.
- **Loyalty** — tier, points and rewards. Loyalty is driven by collected spend
  and is recomputed nightly, so figures reflect real money in.
- **Communications** — the message history with this customer.
- **Documents** — agreements, invoices and uploaded files.
- **Settings** — preferences, plus import/export.

## Who counts as a customer

"Being a customer" means having a customer profile — not having the CUSTOMER
role. Back-office users carry a profile too, so staff can rent vehicles like
anyone else. Don't be surprised to see colleagues in the directory.

## Good practice

- Check **Risk** before handing over an expensive bike.
- Keep verification current; expired licences block check-out for good reason.
- Respect communication **preferences** — don't message someone who has opted
  out.
