# Incidents

An incident is any significant event involving a vehicle: a crash, theft,
serious damage, breakdown or a safety event. Logging it creates a tracked case
you can follow through to resolution, separate from the routine damage you settle
at check-in.

## When to log an incident

- The vehicle was in an accident (whether or not the customer was at fault).
- The vehicle was stolen or went missing.
- There's major damage beyond normal wear, or a safety defect.
- Anything that may involve insurance, police or a claim.

## Logging one

1. Open **Incidents** and create a record, linking it to the vehicle and (where
   relevant) the booking and customer.
2. Describe what happened, when and where.
3. Attach photos and any documents (police report, third-party details).
4. Track it through its lifecycle to resolution.

## Incident vs. damage charge

- **Routine damage** (a scratch, a scuff) is priced from the damage tariff and
  settled at **check-in** — no incident needed.
- An **incident** is for the bigger events that need investigation, may involve a
  third party or insurer, and take time to resolve.

When in doubt, log the incident — it's easier to close a case that turned out
minor than to reconstruct a serious one after the fact.
