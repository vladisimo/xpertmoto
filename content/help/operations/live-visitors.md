# Live visitors & analytics

Live Visitors shows what's happening on the public {{siteName}} site right now,
backed by deeper analytics tabs for understanding demand over time. It's most
useful to managers and admins watching marketing and the booking funnel.

## The tabs

- **Live** — visitors on the site this moment, and what they're viewing.
- **Sessions** — individual visit journeys.
- **Interactions** — clicks and events on key elements.
- **Sales performance** — bookings and revenue from web traffic.
- **Overview** — headline metrics in one view.
- **Acquisition** — where visitors come from (channels, campaigns, referrers).
- **Behaviour** — what people do on site (pages, paths).
- **Conversion** — the booking funnel and where visitors drop off.
- **Retention** — whether visitors and customers come back.
- **Alerts** — notable changes worth your attention.

## How to use it

- **Marketing check:** after launching a campaign, watch **Acquisition** and
  **Live** to confirm it's driving real traffic.
- **Funnel health:** if bookings dip, **Conversion** shows the step where people
  abandon — often a fixable friction point.
- **Channel quality:** **Acquisition** plus **Sales performance** tells you not
  just who sends traffic, but who sends *paying* traffic.

## A note on the data

Analytics here are aggregated for understanding trends, not for identifying
individuals — visitor PII is minimised and redacted. Use it to improve the
experience and spend marketing wisely, not to profile people.
