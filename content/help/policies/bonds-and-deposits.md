# Bonds & deposits

A bond (security deposit) protects {{siteName}} against damage, loss and extra
charges during a hire. Understanding how it's held and released avoids the most
common customer questions.

## How the bond works

- The bond is taken as a **pre-authorisation** on the customer's card at
  check-out — it is **held, not charged**. The money is ring-fenced on their card
  but doesn't leave their account.
- If the vehicle comes back clean, on time and with nothing to charge, the
  authorisation is **released**.
- If there's a chargeable item (damage, late fee, fuel, tolls, fines), it's
  **captured** against the bond up to the held amount during settlement.

## When the bond is released

After a clean return, the hold is released automatically after a configured
window — typically **14 days** — provided no damage is found. The window exists
to cover charges (like tolls or fines) that can arrive after the hire ends. The
exact period is set in **System Settings**.

## Common questions

- *"Why is there still a hold on my card?"* — The authorisation releases after
  the configured window; how fast it disappears from their statement also depends
  on their bank.
- *"You charged my bond!"* — Point to the settlement: the captured amount maps to
  damage tariff items or specific charges, backed by check-in photos.

## For staff

- The bond is authorised at **check-out** and resolved at **check-in** — see
  those workflow guides.
- Captures and releases keep the booking's balance in step automatically.
- Track bond state under **Finance → Bonds**.
