# Back-office basics

Welcome to the {{siteName}} back office. This guide covers the things every team
member needs, whatever their role: how to find your way around, how to search,
how roles work, and how to sign in securely.

## The two portals

The back office is split into two portals, each with its own accent colour so
you always know where you are:

- **Operations** (green) — the day-to-day staff portal: bookings, fleet,
  customers and communications. Lives under `/staff`.
- **Administration** (indigo) — configuration and oversight: users, finance,
  pricing, integrations and platform tools. Lives under `/admin`.

Which portals you can open depends on your role (see below). The sidebar only
shows the sections you can access.

## Getting around

- The **sidebar** on the left is your main navigation. Hover a multi-tab item
  (like Communications or Reports) to see its tabs in a fly-out.
- Collapse the sidebar with the chevron at the bottom to reclaim screen width;
  icons stay visible with tooltips.
- On a phone or tablet, tap the menu button to open the sidebar as a drawer.
- The **`?`** beside most page titles opens the help article for that page.

## Global search (⌘K)

Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl + K** (Windows) anywhere to open global search.
Start typing to jump straight to:

- **Bookings** by reference, customer name or vehicle rego
- **Vehicles** by rego, internal code, VIN, make or model
- **Customers** by name, email or phone
- **Receipts** and **tax invoices** by number
- **Help & guides** — these articles

It's the fastest way to get anywhere. You don't need to be on a particular page.

## Roles at a glance

There are four back-office roles, each inheriting the access of the ones below it:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | Operations portal: bookings, check-in/out, customers, fleet, comms, support. |
| **Manager** | Everything Staff can, plus AI Insights and richer analytics. |
| **Admin** | Operations *and* the Administration portal: users, finance, pricing, reports, integrations. |
| **Super Admin** | Everything, plus Platform and System Settings — the infrastructure and global configuration. |

If a section isn't in your sidebar, your role doesn't have access to it. Ask an
admin if you think you need it.

## Signing in securely

Every back-office account must use **two-factor authentication (2FA)**. The
first time you sign in after accepting an invite, you'll be guided through
enrolling an authenticator app (TOTP). After that, you'll enter a 6-digit code
from your app at each sign-in.

You can sign in with email + password or, if your organisation allows it, a
linked Google / Microsoft / Apple / GitHub account — 2FA is still enforced
either way.
