# Getting started for staff

This guide walks through a typical front-desk day at {{siteName}}. If you
haven't yet, skim **Back-office basics** first for navigation and sign-in.

## Start your shift on the dashboard

Open **Operations → Dashboard**. It's your command centre for the day:

- **Today** — vehicles due out (pickups) and due back (returns).
- **Overdue** — hires that should already be back; chase these first.
- **Fleet** — what's available, on hire, or in maintenance right now.
- **Support** — open customer tickets that need a reply.

Then check **Priority Tasks** for the work queue — licences to verify, bonds to
release, documents to chase. Each task deep-links to exactly where you action it.

## Handling a pickup (check-out)

When a customer arrives to collect a bike:

1. Open their booking (search by reference or name with **⌘K**).
2. **Verify their licence** — compare the uploaded photos to the physical card
   and tick it off. Expired licences are flagged; don't hand over keys on one.
3. **Allocate a vehicle** if one isn't pre-assigned.
4. **Inspect** the bike and record its condition with photos.
5. **Confirm** the agreement and capture the customer's **signature**.

The full step-by-step is in **Checking a vehicle out**.

## Handling a return (check-in)

When a bike comes back:

1. Open the booking and start the return flow.
2. **Assess** the bike, **inspect** for new damage, and photograph anything
   chargeable.
3. **Settle** — apply any late fees, fuel, tolls or damage, then take payment or
   release the bond.
4. Capture the customer's **signature** to close it out.

See **Checking a vehicle in** for detail, and **Bonds & deposits** for how the
security hold is released.

## Helping customers

- Use **Customers** to look up a profile, history, risk flags and documents.
- Answer questions in **Support** — tickets come in from the customer portal.
- Send messages from **Communications** (email or SMS), individually or to a
  segment.

## Good habits

- Photograph generously at check-out *and* check-in — it protects everyone in a
  damage dispute.
- Never skip licence verification.
- If something's off (a crash, theft, or major damage), log an **Incident**.
- Leave bikes that need a service flagged for **Maintenance** so they drop off
  hire automatically.
