# Getting started for managers

As a manager you can do everything a staff member can, plus a layer of oversight
tools. This guide points you at the parts of the system that help you run the
depot rather than work the front desk.

## Read the room with AI Insights

**Operations → AI Insights** turns the week's data into plain-English
narratives: what's trending, where revenue is leaking, which vehicles are
under-utilised, and emerging risks. Start here on a Monday to get the story
before you dig into numbers.

## Watch live demand

**Operations → Live Visitors** shows real-time site activity plus deeper tabs
for sessions, acquisition, conversion and retention. Use it to:

- see whether a campaign is driving traffic,
- spot drop-off in the booking funnel (Conversion tab),
- understand which channels bring the best customers (Acquisition tab).

## Keep the queue under control

The **Dashboard** and **Priority Tasks** aren't just for staff — use them to see
whether overdue hires, unverified licences or unreleased bonds are piling up. A
growing queue is an early warning sign.

## Support quality

In **Support → Insights** you'll find recurring themes across customer tickets.
If the same question keeps coming up, that's usually a fixable process or content
problem.

## What you can't do (and who can)

Business configuration — pricing, users, depots, finance settings, integrations
— lives in the **Administration** portal and needs an **Admin** role. If you need
a rate changed, a new staff member added, or an integration connected, loop in an
admin. See **Getting started for admins** for what that involves.
